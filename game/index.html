Where these numbers come from

    How the game works

    Dollar amounts come from the sources above. Items tagged Game estimate are derived by us from sourced figures, and each one explains the math. The effects on safety, services, affordability, business and fiscal health, the council vote counts and the year-in-review headlines are simplified game mechanics meant to start a discussion. They are not predictions. Simulated headlines are labeled as simulated.

    The map compresses real geography: the Loop, river, lakefront and landmarks are placed by Chicago’s address grid, with stadiums pulled in closer and building heights exaggerated.
